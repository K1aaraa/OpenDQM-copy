Open Distributed Quality Management (OpenDQM)

What we're about:

Quality management has not yet adapted to the new digital, democratized, distributed reality.

OpenDQM is establishing open, shared, trusted, interoperable ecosystem to support democratized quality control and distributed quality assurance, verification, liability and continuous improvement across heterogeneous actors
